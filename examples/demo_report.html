What this assessment does not do

This is a measurement of alarm system performance against published benchmarks. It is not a rationalisation, and it is not a compliance record.

Deciding whether a specific alarm should be removed, re-ranged, re-prioritised, suppressed or made state-based requires the P&IDs, the HAZOP and LOPA record, the defined operator action and consequence for each alarm, and the people who actually run the plant. Those decisions carry safety consequence and need a competent person who is accountable for them — and under ISA-18.2 and IEC 61511 the rationalisation record must be documented, reviewed and retained.

Treat the bad-actor and chattering lists as a prioritised work queue for that exercise, not as its conclusion.
